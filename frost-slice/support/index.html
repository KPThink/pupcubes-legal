Common questions

How do I play? Swipe across the block to cut it. Ice levels: cut the ice away to free the treasure inside. Order levels: cut the food into the number of equal pieces asked for. Fewer swipes earn more stars.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in. The game plays offline once it is installed.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the game or its data removes it. You can also reset it in Settings.

I bought No Ads or coins on a new device. How do I get them back? Open the shop, go to Coins and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. No Ads comes back; coins already used stay used.

A purchase did not arrive. Close and reopen the game, then tap Restore purchases. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date and the item.

Can I buy mystery items? No. Mystery treasures are only earned by playing.

Can I play without ads? Rewarded ads are always optional. The one-time No Ads purchase removes the ads shown between levels. Zen mode has no ads.

How do I change sound, vibration or ad choices? Open Settings from the home screen.
