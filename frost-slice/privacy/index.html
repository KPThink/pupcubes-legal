Frost Slice Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Developer: KP Think Private Limited (“we”, “us”) · Contact: kpthinkspace@gmail.com

In short. Frost Slice is a slicing game that works offline. It has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data. Your progress, coins and collection are saved on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

Data stored on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, collected items, stall level, settings and purchase status are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. You can also delete them in the game with Settings > Reset progress.

Gameplay statistics

This version of the game counts some gameplay events (for example level started or completed) only on your device. They are not sent to us or to any analytics service. If a future version sends anonymous gameplay statistics, we will update this policy first and you will be able to turn it off with Settings > Share usage data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example to get more swipes after a failed level, double your coins, get a hammer or free coins) and, from level 5, an occasional full-screen ad after a won level. There are no ads in Zen mode. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required (including some US states), Google’s consent tool asks for your choice before personalised ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time with Settings > Ad privacy choices (shown where required). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). See how Google uses data from ads. The “No Ads” purchase removes the ads between levels; ads you choose to watch stay available.

Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)

Coin packs and “No Ads” are processed by Google Play (or the App Store). We use RevenueCat to check and restore your purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information and basic device information. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Mystery items in the game are earned by playing and cannot be bought with money.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not collect data from your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not ask for an account, name or email address to play.

Children

Frost Slice is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the services described above, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your choices and rights

You can skip all rewarded ads, change your ad choices, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, reset your progress in Settings, or ask us about, or request deletion of, purchase records by emailing kpthinkspace@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, India or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us to exercise them.

Security and changes

Traffic to the ad and purchase services is encrypted in transit. If a future version adds features that handle more data (for example analytics, optional accounts or cloud save), we will update this policy before releasing them. The effective date above shows the latest version.
