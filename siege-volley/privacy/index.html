Siege Volley Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Developer: KP Think Private Limited (“we”, “us”) · Contact: kpthinkspace@gmail.com

In short. Siege Volley is a game that works offline. It has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect any personal data. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. This version of the game contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your stage progress, stars, coins, launchers, bombs, upgrades, chosen difficulty and settings (sound, vibration, graphics) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data or using Settings > Reset progress deletes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools in this version.
  • We do not show ads in this version, and we do not use an advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Permissions

The app uses the internet permission that every Android web-view app has, and vibration for short feedback during battles. It does not request location, storage, camera, microphone or contacts access.

Shop and rewards

In this version, shop items and “watch an ad” buttons are test placeholders: no money is charged and no real ads are shown.

Future versions

If a later version adds advertising (for example Google AdMob), in-app purchases (Google Play Billing, App Store, RevenueCat) or analytics, we will update this policy and the store listing before that version is released, describe what data those services process, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Children

Siege Volley is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and change the effective date above.
