Barrel Yard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Developer: KP Think Private Limited

Barrel Yard is an offline action game for Android and iPhone. This page explains what the game does with information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your mission progress, stars, achievements and settings are saved only on your own device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Permissions

The only permission the game uses is vibration, for haptic feedback during play. Android may also list the standard network access that comes with the app framework, but the game does not use it to send data. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Internet

The game works fully offline and does not send data over the internet.

Children

Barrel Yard is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this (for example analytics or ads), this page and the store listing will be updated before that version is released.
