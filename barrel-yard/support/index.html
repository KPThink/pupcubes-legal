Common questions

Do I need internet or an account? No. The game works fully offline and has no sign-in.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the game or its data removes it.

How do I use slow motion? Fill the focus bar (FX) by fighting, then tap FOCUS (or press F on a keyboard).
