Pupcubes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Developer: KP Think Private Limited (“we”, “us”) · Contact: kpthinkspace@gmail.com

In short. Pupcubes is a puzzle game that works offline. It has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or microphone data. Your progress is saved only on your device. If you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad, Google AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID. If you buy “Remove ads”, the purchase is handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, coins, stars, collected pictures, settings and purchase status are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded ads. An ad is shown only if you tap a button to watch one; no ad appears on its own and nothing in the game requires you to watch an ad. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are enabled, AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Ads are non-personalised. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required (including some US states), Google’s consent tool asks for a choice before any ad is requested. If the choice is declined, or the tool cannot load, no ad is shown, and the game works the same. You can review or change your choice at any time with the “Privacy & ad choices” button in the Shop (shown where required). You can also reset or disable your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). See how Google uses data from ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)

The one-time “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check and restore your purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information and basic device information. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run our own analytics or crash reporting.
  • We do not collect data from your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Children

Pupcubes is made for a general audience aged 9 and over, which includes children. We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, locations or any other personal information from any player. Because children may play, we treat every player as a child for advertising: ads in Pupcubes are requested as child-directed, are limited to a General (G) content rating, and are always non-personalised, so your advertising ID is not used to tailor ads. Ads are shown only if a player taps to watch one. We use only Google-certified advertising services. Parents and guardians can contact us at kpthinkspace@gmail.com with any question, or to ask about or request deletion of data held by our service providers.

Your choices and rights

You can skip all ads, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, or ask us about, or request deletion of, purchase records held by RevenueCat by emailing kpthinkspace@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, India or California) you, or your parent or guardian, may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us to exercise them. Because the game stores your progress only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Security and changes

Traffic to the ad and purchase services is encrypted in transit. If a future version adds features that handle more data (for example optional accounts or cloud backup), we will update this policy before releasing them. The effective date above shows the latest version.
