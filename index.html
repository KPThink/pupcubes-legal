Pupcubes Support

Developer: KP Think Private Limited

Pupcubes is a calm pixel-art colour puzzle: send pups round the conveyor belt to shoot matching cubes and fill in the picture. Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email us and we will reply as soon as we can.

Contact

kpthinkspace@gmail.com

If you are reporting a problem, please include your device model, system version, the level number and what happened.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap a pup to send it onto the belt. Each pup shoots the cubes of its own colour that it can reach. Fill the whole picture to win. A short tutorial appears the first time you play.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in, and the game plays offline.

Where is my progress saved? On your device, automatically. Deleting the game or its data removes it.

Are there ads or purchases? This version has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Some colours look alike to me. Turn on the colour-blind option in Settings to show a symbol on every colour.
