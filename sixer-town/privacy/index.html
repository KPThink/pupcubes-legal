Sixer Town Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Developer: KP Think Private Limited (“we”, “us”) · Contact: kpthinkspace@gmail.com

In short. Sixer Town is a cricket game that works offline. It has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data. Your club, progress and settings are saved on your device, and the game’s own play statistics stay on your device too. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

Data stored on your device

Your club name, stars, coins, bats, kits, ground upgrades, trophies, settings, the age range you choose and your purchase status are stored locally on your device. The game also keeps a local record of play events (for example matches played and shot timing) to tune difficulty and to build the optional play report described below. This record is not sent to us or to any analytics service. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data or using Settings > Reset all progress deletes it.

Optional play report

If you tap Share feel test report in Settings or Share my stats in the Trophy room, the game opens your device’s share sheet with a short text summary (your club name and stats, or timing results with your device platform and system version). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with, and it then goes only where you send it.

Your age range

When you start, the game asks whether you are under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older. The answer stays on your device and is only used to set how ads are requested: players under 18 never get personalised ads, and players under 13 only get ads that are marked as child-directed and rated suitable for general audiences. You can change it at any time in Settings > Player age.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads (only when you tap to watch one, for example for free coins or to double a match reward) and, now and then, a short ad break between matches. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. For players under 13 the game asks AdMob to treat requests as child-directed; for players under 18 it asks AdMob not to personalise ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required (including some US states), Google’s consent tool asks for your choice before personalised ads are requested. Where this applies you can review or change your choice at any time with Settings > Privacy options (ads consent). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). See how Google uses data from ads. The “No Forced Ads” purchase removes the ad breaks between matches.

Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)

The Starter Pack, “No Forced Ads” and coin packs are processed by Google Play (or the App Store). We use RevenueCat to check and restore your purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information and basic device information. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not send your gameplay to any analytics service.
  • We do not collect data from your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not ask for an account, name or email address to play.

Children

The game is designed so that a child can play without giving us any personal information: there are no accounts, no chat and no gameplay analytics, and ads for players who say they are under 13 are requested as child-directed, non-personalised and family-safe. Purchases go through Google Play, which applies its own family and parental controls. If you are a parent and believe your child’s data has been collected through the services described above, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your choices and rights

You can skip all rewarded ads, change your age range, change your ad choices where offered, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, delete your progress with Settings > Reset all progress, or ask us about, or request deletion of, purchase records by emailing kpthinkspace@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, India or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us to exercise them.

Security and changes

Traffic to the ad and purchase services is encrypted in transit. If a future version adds features that handle more data (for example analytics, optional accounts or cloud save), we will update this policy before releasing them. The effective date above shows the latest version.
