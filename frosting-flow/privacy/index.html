Frosting Flow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Developer: KP Think Private Limited (“we”, “us”) · Contact: kpthinkspace@gmail.com

In short. Frosting Flow is a puzzle game that works offline. It has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data. Your progress is saved on your device. The game sends anonymous gameplay events to Mixpanel so we can improve the levels (you can turn this off in Settings). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, coins, stars, settings and purchase status are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Gameplay analytics (Mixpanel)

To see which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Mixpanel: for example app opened, level started, won or failed, hints, offers shown, ads shown and in-game purchases, together with the app version, the platform (Android or iOS) and a random ID created when the app is first installed. This ID is not your advertising ID or any device identifier, and it is not linked to your name or email. Mixpanel may use the network address of the request to estimate your approximate country and city. You can stop sending these events at any time with Settings > Share usage data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads (for example to get an extra slot after a failed level or to double your coins) and, from level 15, an occasional full-screen ad between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required (including some US states), Google’s consent tool asks for your choice before personalised ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time with Settings > Privacy and ad choices (shown where required). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). See how Google uses data from ads. The “No Ads” purchase removes the between-level ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)

Coin packs and “No Ads” are processed by Google Play (or the App Store). We use RevenueCat to check and restore your purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information and basic device information. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not collect data from your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not ask for an account, name or email address to play.

Children

Frosting Flow is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the services described above, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off usage data in Settings, skip all rewarded ads, change your ad choices, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, or ask us about, or request deletion of, analytics or purchase records by emailing kpthinkspace@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, India or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us to exercise them.

Security and changes

Traffic to the analytics, ad and purchase services is encrypted in transit. If a future version adds features that handle more data (for example optional accounts or cloud save), we will update this policy before releasing them. The effective date above shows the latest version.
