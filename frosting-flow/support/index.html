Common questions

How do I play? Tap the front piping bag in a lane to send it around the belt. Each bag frosts the first matching cell it sees. Bags with frosting left after a full lap wait in the tray; if the tray fills up, the level is lost.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in. Levels play offline once the game is installed.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the game or its data removes it.

I bought No Ads or coins on a new device. How do I get them back? Open the shop and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. No Ads comes back; coins already used stay used.

A purchase did not arrive. Close and reopen the game, then tap Restore purchases. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date and the item.

Can I play without ads? Rewarded ads are always optional. The one-time No Ads purchase removes the ads shown between levels.

How do I change sound, vibration or ad choices? Open Settings from the home screen.
